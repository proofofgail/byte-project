Privacy

Last updated October 2026

Who we are

The Byte Project is a curatorial platform based in London. If you have any question about this notice or your information, write to hello@byte-project.com.

What we collect

When you sign up on this website, we collect your email address and the date you signed up. We do not collect anything else, and this site does not use tracking cookies.

Why we collect it

We use your email address only to send you news, projects and releases from The Byte Project. We rely on your consent to do this, which you give by signing up, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Where it is kept

Sign ups are stored in a private Google Sheet, so Google acts as our service provider. When we begin sending newsletters, your address will be moved to an email newsletter service used solely to send them. We will never sell your information or share it with anyone else for their own use.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can unsubscribe at any time by emailing hello@byte-project.com or using the link in any email we send. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
